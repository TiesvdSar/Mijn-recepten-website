Pici with Anchovy & Breadcrumb
A Dutch restaurant-style main course: hand-rolled pici pasta tossed with a crispy anchovy, garlic, chili and sourdough breadcrumb mixture, finished with pecorino. Lactose-free. Prep ±15 min (excluding making the pici dough itself).

INGREDIENTS
• 2 portions cooked pici pasta (see separate pici recipe)
• 125 milliliters extra virgin olive oil
• 2 garlic cloves, pressed
• 8 anchovy fillets, drained (doubled per handwritten note on card)
• 1 teaspoons chili flakes
• 50 grams fresh sourdough breadcrumbs
• 30 grams pecorino, finely grated, for sprinkling (amount to taste — card gives no exact quantity)

STEPS
1. Make the pici: Make the pici according to the separate pici recipe (this card references it as a different page in the cookbook, not shown here).
2. Sauté garlic and anchovy: Heat 125 milliliters extra virgin olive oil in a large, deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Sauté the 2 garlic cloves, pressed and 8 anchovy fillets, drained (doubled per handwritten note on card) for 4-5 minutes, until the garlic is lightly golden and soft and the anchovy has dissolved into the oil.
3. Toast the breadcrumb mixture: Add the 1 teaspoons chili flakes and 50 grams fresh sourdough breadcrumbs to the pan. Stir-fry for about 5 minutes, or until crispy and deep golden brown.
4. Boil the pici: Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to the boil (1 tsp salt per liter of water). Cook the pici for 3-4 minutes (timing depends on thickness) until al dente. The pici can be slightly firm or even a bit chewy, but shouldn't taste floury.
5. Combine and serve: Drain the pasta, let it drain briefly, then toss it through the anchovy and garlic mixture. Sprinkle with the 30 grams pecorino, finely grated, for sprinkling (amount to taste — card gives no exact quantity) and serve immediately.

NOTES
Per person: ±541 kcal / 12g protein / 36g fat / 41g carbs (as printed on the card).

TIP from the card: Don't make this pasta too far ahead, as the pici can stick together or dry out too much. If you want to prep something in advance, make the anchovy/garlic/breadcrumb sauce ahead — it keeps well in an airtight jar in the fridge for a few days.
